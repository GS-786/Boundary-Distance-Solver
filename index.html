Paste text

Reading tips

  • After reading, you see every row it found. You choose which column is the ID, Easting, Northing and level, then Replace or Append.
  • If your schedule lists Northing before Easting, tick Swap Easting / Northing in the review.
  • For photos, a straight, well-lit shot reads best. You can correct the recognised text before you import it.
  • A PDF or image you import can also become the drawing underlay in Measure, so you can measure straight off the drawing.
