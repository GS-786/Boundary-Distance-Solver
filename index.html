Reading tips

  • After reading, you see every row it found. You choose which column is the ID, Easting, Northing and level, then Replace or Append.
  • If your schedule lists Northing before Easting, tick Swap Easting / Northing in the review.
  • For photos, a straight, well-lit shot reads best. You can correct the recognised text before you import it.
  • A PDF or image you import can also become the drawing underlay in Measure, so you can measure straight off the drawing.

Export

On the layout sheet

Every export includes the plot corners (P1, P2…) at their coordinates. DXF is at true coordinates in the project system, with a coloured layer per category, road class and land use, so it overlays the boundary DXF. The colour map PNG is the current map view with the street or satellite map underneath and see-through colours on top; the SVG is the same overlay as a vector drawing. KML opens in Google Earth and Google My Maps. CSV opens in Excel.

1DXF: exact. True geometry and coordinates, to the millimetre. Best choice.
2Corner coordinates: exact. Paste the Easting and Northing of each corner.
3PDF: good. Traced over the drawing after calibrating it. As accurate as the calibration, usually a few centimetres.
4Image / photo: approximate. Traced the same way, limited by the picture's resolution and distortion.
